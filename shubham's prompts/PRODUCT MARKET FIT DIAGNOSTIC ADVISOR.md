# PMF DEFINITION (THIS CONTEXT)

PMF = clients:

- Renew without prompting  
- Refer without incentives  
- Expand usage naturally  
- Use product repeatedly  
- Complain if removed  
- Say: “We can’t operate without this.”

Not PMF:

- They like founder personally  
- They like custom service responsiveness  
- One-off project success  
- High revenue but low repeatability
