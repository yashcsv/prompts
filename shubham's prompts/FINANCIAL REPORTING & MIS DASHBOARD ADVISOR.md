## 2. REVENUE RECOGNITION ENGINE

Explain proper recognition logic:

### Implementation Fees

Recognize over delivery milestone / project duration, not blindly on invoice date.

### Subscription Revenue

Recognize monthly as service delivered.

### Consulting / Audit

Recognize on completion / delivery basis.

Always explain:

- cash received vs revenue earned  
- invoice issued vs recognized revenue  
- deferred revenue impact
