# 1. INTERVIEW DESIGN (30 MIN MASTER GUIDE)

Build a structured 30-minute guide.

## Segment A — Context (5 min)

Ask:

- Company size?
- Revenue band?
- Team structure?
- Role responsibilities?
- Current tools?
- What metrics are you responsible for?

## Segment B — Pain Exploration (10 min)

Ask:

- Top 3 operational headaches?
- Which consumes most time?
- What does it cost monthly/yearly?
- How often does it happen?
- Who feels it most?
- Tell me last time it happened.

## Segment C — Existing Solutions (5 min)

Ask:

- How do you solve it today?
- What tools/vendors tried?
- Why didn’t it stick?
- Why not solved already?

## Segment D — Urgency Probe (5 min)

Ask:

- If still unresolved in 12 months, what happens?
- Why fix now vs later?
- What triggered attention recently?

## Segment E — Buying Process (5 min)

Ask:

- Who else involved?
- Budget owner?
- Approval path?
- What earns vendor trust?
- What kills deals?

# 2. HIGH-SIGNAL QUESTION BANK (MANDATORY)

# 4. INTERVIEW FACILITATION SYSTEM

Teach 5 principles:

## Stay Curious, Not Salesy

No pitching.

## Follow Energy

If emotion rises, dig deeper.

## Use Silence

Wait 5 seconds.

## Reflect Back

“Did I understand correctly?”

## Ask What Else

Get second-layer truth.

Add:

## Red Flags to Avoid

- Leading question
- Defending your idea
- Talking too much
- Solving mid-call
- Accepting vague answers

# 5. LIVE INTERVIEW TACTICS

If respondent says vague statement:

Generate probes:

- Can you give example?
- How often exactly?
- What happened last week?
- What did that cost?
- Who noticed first?
- Why unresolved?

If respondent guarded:

- Use peer framing
- Ask process not politics
- Normalize pain
