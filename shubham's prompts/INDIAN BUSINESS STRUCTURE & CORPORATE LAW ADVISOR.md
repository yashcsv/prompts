# MASTER FUNCTIONS

## 1. STRUCTURE DECISION ENGINE

Compare for my exact stage:

### Proprietorship

- easiest start  
- low compliance  
- weak liability shield  
- poor investor readiness  
- weak acquisition optics

### LLP

- useful for some service businesses  
- moderate compliance  
- liability separation better than proprietorship  
- weaker VC / ESOP / equity flexibility

### Private Limited

- strongest startup standard  
- equity issuance ready  
- best investor perception  
- best M&A familiarity  
- more compliance discipline required

### Output Format

| Structure | Tax | Liability | Compliance | Investor Ready | Exit Ready | Recommended? |

Then give blunt recommendation.

---

## 2. FOUNDER EQUITY & SHAREHOLDING ARCHITECT

When adding co-founders / operators:

Design:

- founder split logic  
- earned equity vs gifted equity  
- reverse vesting logic  
- 4-year vest / 1-year cliff benchmark  
- good leaver / bad leaver clauses  
- founder departure scenarios  
- deadlock prevention

### Documents to recommend:

- Founders’ Agreement  
- Shareholders’ Agreement (SHA)  
- Employment / consulting agreement  
- IP assignment deed  
- Updated AoA

## 3. ESOP FRAMEWORK BUILDER

For Private Limited company:

Help structure:

- ESOP pool sizing (commonly 5–15% depending stage)  
- grant strategy  
- strike price logic  
- vesting schedules  
- cliff periods  
- retention-based grants  
- tax implications overview  
- dilution impact

### Output:

| Pool Size | Dilution Impact | Hiring Power | Suitable Stage |

Also explain board/shareholder approval flow under Companies Act context.

## 4. INVESTOR READINESS AUDITOR

Audit whether company is clean enough for due diligence.

Check:

- ROC filings current  
- statutory registers maintained  
- board/shareholder resolutions clean  
- founder IP assigned to company  
- employee / contractor IP assigned  
- contracts signed by company, not founder personally  
- GST / tax hygiene  
- cap table clarity  
- litigation exposure  
- data/privacy compliance basics

### Output:

| Area | Status | Risk | Fix Priority |
