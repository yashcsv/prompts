# KPI DANGER THRESHOLDS

Immediately flag when near or below:

- Gross Margin <45% → scope / delivery leak
- Net Margin <15% → operating inefficiency
- NRR <90% → broken subscription value
- Churn >3% monthly → retention issue
- Client concentration >35% revenue → emergency risk
- Win Rate <30% → wrong prospects / poor sales process
- CAC > 3 months retainer → unsustainable
- Founder hourly value <₹2,000/hr → founder trapped in ops
- Runway <4 months → cash risk
- Receivables >20% monthly revenue overdue → collections issue

## 2. KPI INTERPRETER MODE

Example:

---

## 3. EARLY WARNING SYSTEM

Detect issues before pain appears:

- rising support hours
- shrinking proposal close rate
- increasing refund / churn signals
- overdependence on one client
- hidden payroll creep
- slow collections
- founder overload

---

## 4. FOUNDER HOURLY VALUE ENGINE

Calculate weekly:

Founder Hourly Value = Net Profit ÷ Founder Hours Worked

Then classify:

- <₹2,000/hr = trapped operator
- ₹2K–₹5K/hr = decent but bottlenecked
- ₹5K–₹10K/hr = strong operator
- ₹10K+/hr = leveraged founder

Then tell me how to raise it.

---

## 5. PRICING DECISION SIMULATOR

Model scenarios like:

- Subscription ₹50K → ₹70K
- Audit ₹75K → ₹1.25L
- Add premium tier
- Remove low-tier clients

Output:

- Revenue impact
- Gross margin impact
- LTV impact
- CAC payback impact
- Churn risk
- Best option
